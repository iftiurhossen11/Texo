# Day-1 HR Onboarding and PPE Policy

**Department:** Human Resources and EHS  
**Document type:** Policy  
**Applies to:** New floor workers, agency workers, contractors, and supervisors  
**Factory context:** Bangladesh garment production floor  
**Effective date:** 28 October 2026  

## 1. Policy Statement

Every new floor worker must complete safety orientation and receive task-appropriate personal protective equipment (PPE) before entering a production area. No production target takes priority over worker safety, dignity, or legal rights.

## 2. Day-1 Onboarding Sequence

1. HR verifies identity, emergency contact, job assignment, and attendance-system registration.
2. EHS delivers a Bangla-and-English briefing on emergency exits, fire alarms, first aid, machine hazards, sharp tools, and safe reporting.
3. The worker receives and signs for the PPE required by the task.
4. A supervisor demonstrates safe work and confirms that the worker understands the procedure.
5. The worker shadows an experienced operator before working independently.
6. HR files the signed acknowledgement and PPE issue record.

## 3. Required PPE

The exact issue depends on the risk assessment and assigned workstation. Standard items may include:

- Safety shoes or approved protective footwear
- Hairnet and mask in areas requiring hygiene or fibre control
- Cut-resistant gloves for cutting, trimming, or sharp-tool work
- Safety glasses for grinding, trimming, chemical, or splash-risk tasks
- Hearing protection in designated high-noise areas
- Apron, sleeve protection, or other task-specific protection

The issue record must include worker ID, item, size, issue date, condition, and acknowledgement. Workers must be trained to wear, inspect, clean, store, and replace PPE. Damaged or missing PPE must be reported immediately.

## 4. Worker Rights

Workers have the right to report hazards, injuries, near misses, harassment, wage concerns, or unsafe instructions without fear of dismissal, discrimination, loss of overtime, or retaliation. Reports may be made to a supervisor, HR, EHS, worker representative, grievance box, or confidential hotline.

Reports must be acknowledged and investigated promptly. Information should be handled confidentially as far as practical. Retaliation is a policy violation and may result in disciplinary action. Workers may request a correction if an attendance or training record is inaccurate.

## 5. Attendance and First-Week Follow-up

Workers must clock in and out using the approved system and notify HR or the supervisor before a shift if they will be late or absent. Supervisors must not alter records to hide absence, overtime, or incidents. EHS shall conduct a follow-up check during the first week to confirm PPE fit, safe practice, and unanswered questions.
