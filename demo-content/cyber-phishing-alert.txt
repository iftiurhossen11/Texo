CYBERSECURITY AWARENESS ALERT: PHISHING EMAILS
================================================

Department: Cybersecurity and IT
Document type: Policy
Audience: All factory employees, supervisors, office staff, and contractors
Review date: 28 October 2026

WHAT IS PHISHING?
-----------------

Phishing is a deceptive email or message designed to steal passwords, OTPs, payment information, buyer data, or access to factory systems. Attackers may pretend to be a buyer, bank, HR, courier, manager, or IT support.

WARNING SIGNS
-------------

- Urgent action required: the message threatens account closure, payroll delay, or disciplinary action if you do not act immediately.
- Spelling mistakes, unusual grammar, poor formatting, or a greeting that does not match the sender.
- A suspicious sender address, look-alike domain, or reply-to address different from the visible sender.
- Unexpected attachments, especially ZIP files, executable files, macro-enabled documents, or password-protected archives.
- Links that do not match the displayed text, shortened URLs, or requests to enter a password after clicking.
- Requests for confidential buyer information, payroll data, gift cards, bank changes, or an unusual payment.

SAFE RESPONSE
------------

1. Stop and verify the request using a known phone number or a separate trusted channel.
2. Do not open unexpected attachments or enable macros.
3. Do not enter your password, OTP, or payment details into an unexpected link.
4. If you receive a suspicious email, do not click any links. Forward it to IT-Security@factory.com immediately.
5. If you clicked a link or entered information, contact IT Security immediately and preserve the original message.
6. Report suspected phishing quickly. Early reporting protects co-workers and production systems.

IT Security will not punish a worker for reporting a suspicious message in good faith. Never share passwords by email, messaging app, or phone; IT Security will never ask for your password.
