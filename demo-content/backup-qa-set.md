# Texo — Backup Q&A Set (Exhibition Demo)

Use these if time allows after the primary "broken stitch" demo. All answers are grounded in `garment-quality-inspection-sop.docx` sections 4–9.

## QA (primary — always rehearse first)
**Q:** What should an inspector do for a broken stitch?
**Expected:** Stop line, tag BS-01, rework bin, QC log, notify supervisor if >3/hour.

## Production (Section 5)
**Q:** What should happen at the inspection point when a defective piece is found?
**Expected:** Red line-stop button, hold cage, call line leader within 2 minutes, resume only after clearance.

## Compliance / Sustainability (Section 6)
**Q:** What AQL should we use for major defects during a buyer audit?
**Expected:** General inspection level II, major defects AQL 2.5, minor 4.0; keep 30 days of QC logs ready.

## HR & Training (Section 7)
**Q:** What must a new floor employee complete on their first day?
**Expected:** 30-minute safety briefing, sign acknowledgment form, shadow one full shift, report injuries immediately.

## Supply Chain & Merchandising (Section 8)
**Q:** What checks are required when raw materials arrive at the factory gate?
**Expected:** Delivery note vs PO, swatch/GSM check ±5%, reject shade mismatch, log in material inward register.

## IT & Cybersecurity (Section 9)
**Q:** What is the first step if an employee receives a suspicious phishing email?
**Expected:** Do not click links; disconnect from Wi-Fi if already clicked; forward to it-security@factory.local.
