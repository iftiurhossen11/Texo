BUYER COMPLIANCE REQUIREMENTS AND AUDIT PREPARATION
====================================================

Department: Compliance and Sustainability
Document type: Buyer Requirement
Factory context: Bangladesh ready-made garment manufacturing
Review date: 28 October 2026

1. PURPOSE
---------

This operational brief helps factory managers prepare evidence for buyer social-compliance, health-and-safety, and responsible-sourcing reviews. It should be read with the current buyer manual, Bangladesh law, and the factory's approved policies.

2. H&M-ALIGNED RESPONSIBLE SOURCING EXPECTATIONS
------------------------------------------------

The factory shall demonstrate a fair, safe, and respectful workplace consistent with H&M sustainability expectations:

- Fair wages: pay regular wages and approved overtime accurately and on time; maintain payroll, attendance, leave, deduction, and benefit records.
- Safe working environment: maintain clear emergency exits, fire systems, machine guards, first aid, drinking water, sanitation, lighting, ventilation, and safe chemical storage.
- No child labor: verify age and identity documents during recruitment, protect personnel records, and prevent employment below the legal minimum age.
- Respectful treatment: prohibit forced labor, harassment, discrimination, violence, intimidation, and retaliation.
- Environmental responsibility: control wastewater, waste segregation, chemical handling, energy use, and environmental permits according to the site plan.

3. ZARA-ALIGNED SUPPLIER CODE REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------------------------

Suppliers supporting Zara must protect worker rights and maintain a lawful workplace:

- Respect freedom of association, lawful worker representation, and access to a grievance mechanism.
- Provide written contracts, legal wages, lawful working hours, statutory leave, and accurate overtime payment.
- Complete documented risk assessments for production floors, boiler rooms, chemical stores, warehouses, and dormitories where applicable.
- Keep fire exits, alarms, evacuation routes, drills, emergency teams, and first-aid resources ready.
- Provide suitable PPE, safety training, accident reporting, medical response, and corrective action.
- Apply equivalent standards to approved subcontractors and homeworking arrangements.

4. AUDIT PREPARATION CHECKLIST FOR MANAGERS
--------------------------------------------

[ ] Confirm the current H&M, Zara, and factory policy versions are available.
[ ] Reconcile the worker list, age documents, contracts, attendance, payroll, overtime, leave, and benefits.
[ ] Verify that wage calculations and deductions can be traced to payroll or bank records.
[ ] Review fire, electrical, building, chemical, boiler, machine-safety, and first-aid inspection records.
[ ] Walk every production area: exits clear, extinguishers accessible, guards installed, aisles unobstructed.
[ ] Confirm PPE issue records and safety-training attendance for new and existing workers.
[ ] Review incident, near-miss, grievance, corrective-action, and non-retaliation records.
[ ] Verify approved subcontractors, purchase orders, production records, and authorization documents.
[ ] Prepare translated notices and ensure workers can participate in private interviews.
[ ] Close overdue corrective actions and keep objective evidence such as photos, invoices, and training logs.
[ ] Brief supervisors not to coach, threaten, or retaliate against workers during interviews.
[ ] Assign an evidence coordinator and an auditor escort; keep original records available.

This document is a demonstration aid and does not replace a buyer's latest code of conduct, legal advice, or an independent audit.
