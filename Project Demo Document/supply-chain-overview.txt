TEXO FACTORY SUPPLY CHAIN AND MERCHANDISING - DEPARTMENT GUIDE

Purpose
Supply Chain coordinates the movement of materials and information from supplier commitments through production and delivery. Merchandising connects customer specifications and approvals to sourcing, costing, sampling, planning, and order milestones.

Typical roles
Supply Chain Managers coordinate sourcing and logistics; purchasing officers obtain and compare supplier offers; material planners monitor demand and lead times; merchandisers own buyer requirements and approvals; and warehouse teams confirm receipt and stock status.

Order control
Translate approved demand into a bill of materials and required dates; confirm supplier, specification, quantity, price, and delivery terms; track purchase orders and inbound shipments; receive and inspect material; and reconcile shortages or quality holds before production release. Track changes and communicate schedule risks early.
