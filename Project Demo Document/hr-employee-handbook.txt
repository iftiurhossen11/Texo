TEXO HR EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK

Department: Human Resources

LEAVE REQUESTS
Employees should submit planned leave requests to their department manager and HR before the leave begins. Emergency leave must be reported to the manager as soon as reasonably possible. HR maintains the official leave record and can answer questions about leave balances and holidays.

ATTENDANCE
Employees should arrive on time for their assigned shift and notify their manager promptly if they will be late or absent. Repeated attendance issues should be discussed with the manager and HR through the approved workplace process.

ONBOARDING
New employees should complete their HR orientation, review department safety procedures, confirm their attendance schedule, and ask questions about workplace policies during their first day.

WORKPLACE CONDUCT
All employees are expected to treat colleagues respectfully, follow workplace safety rules, protect confidential information, and report harassment, discrimination, or unsafe conditions to their manager or HR.

CONTACT HR
For questions about leave, attendance, onboarding, workplace conduct, or employee records, contact the HR Manager through the factory's approved HR communication channel.
