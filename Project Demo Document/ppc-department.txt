TEXO FACTORY PRODUCTION PLANNING AND CONTROL - OPERATING GUIDE

Purpose
PPC translates confirmed customer demand into a feasible plan for people, materials, machines, and production time. Plans should account for order priority, approved lead times, available capacity, material readiness, process route, changeovers, and required delivery dates.

Typical roles
The PPC Manager owns the planning horizon and escalation; planners prepare line or process schedules; material coordinators check purchasing and store readiness; and production coordinators compare actual progress with the plan. Sales, merchandising, purchasing, maintenance, and QA supply current constraints and approvals.

Planning cycle
Validate the order and product version; calculate required quantity and process sequence; check capacity and material availability; allocate work to suitable lines or machines; publish a controlled schedule; and review progress at agreed intervals. Record plan revisions and communicate the reason and impact to affected teams.

When output falls behind, verify actual status, identify the constraint, and evaluate authorized recovery options. Do not hide a capacity or quality problem by changing dates without approval. Use completion, backlog, downtime, and material-readiness data to improve the next plan.
