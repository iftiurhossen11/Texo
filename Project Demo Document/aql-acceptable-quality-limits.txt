Texo Quality Reference: Acceptable Quality Limits (AQL)
Department: QA

According to the BKMEA Quality Control Standard for RMG:
- Major defects: The AQL for major defects is less than 2.5% of the inspected items.
- Minor defects: The AQL for minor defects is less than 4.0% of the inspected items.
- Critical defects: Zero tolerance. Stop the lot immediately and notify the QA Manager.

If a lot exceeds these limits, place it on hold for root cause analysis and re-inspection.
