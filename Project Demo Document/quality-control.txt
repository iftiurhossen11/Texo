TEXO FACTORY QUALITY CONTROL - OPERATING GUIDE

Purpose
Quality Control provides objective evidence that a lot or process meets the approved buyer and factory specification. Inspection plans, defect classifications, sampling levels, and acceptance criteria must come from the controlled order documents and applicable quality manual; never invent or substitute a limit.

Roles
The QA Manager approves the inspection plan and nonconformance disposition. Incoming inspectors check fabric, yarn, trims, and packaging; in-line inspectors monitor defined process points; final inspectors verify finished goods; and laboratory staff conduct required tests using controlled methods and calibrated equipment.

Inspection procedure
Identify the order, style, lot, quantity, and specification revision. Confirm sampling instructions and inspection tools; inspect the required sample; record each defect using the approved classification; and distinguish observed facts from conclusions. Segregate suspect goods and notify the responsible supervisor and QA decision-maker.

For nonconforming output, open a traceable report, record containment, investigate root cause with process owners, document corrective action, and re-inspect under the approved plan. Release, rework, or rejection requires authorized disposition. Retain samples, test results, calibration records, and lot records as required.
