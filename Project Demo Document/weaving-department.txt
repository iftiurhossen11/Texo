TEXO FACTORY WEAVING - OPERATING GUIDE

Purpose
Weaving forms fabric by interlacing two yarn systems at approximately right angles: lengthwise warp and crosswise weft. The pattern of interlacement affects fabric properties and appearance. Loom type, weave construction, yarn, and buyer requirements determine the approved process route.

Roles
The Weaving Manager allocates looms and verifies the construction; supervisors control warp preparation and loom readiness; technicians handle setup and maintenance; operators monitor yarn supply, loom stops, and fabric formation; and inspectors identify defects and roll status.

Controlled process
Verify warp and weft yarn codes, lot traceability, draft and weave plan, loom assignment, target width, and approved sample. Check beam and thread-up condition, start with a controlled first-off inspection, and record authorized settings. Monitor broken ends, picks, contamination, width variation, and other listed defects; mark and segregate affected fabric for QA disposition.
