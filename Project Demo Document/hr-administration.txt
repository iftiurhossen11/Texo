TEXO FACTORY HUMAN RESOURCES - ADMINISTRATION GUIDE

Purpose
HR Administration supports the employment relationship and employee services across the factory. Work includes approved recruitment and onboarding, attendance and leave administration, maintenance of employment records, payroll inputs, benefits coordination, training records, employee relations, and offboarding. Local labor law and the factory's approved policies take precedence.

Typical roles
The HR Manager owns policy administration and escalation; HR Officers handle recruitment, attendance, leave, and employee queries; payroll administrators reconcile authorized data with Finance; and welfare or training staff coordinate employee support and learning records.

Documented process
For onboarding, verify approved hiring documents, explain site rules and reporting channels, arrange role-specific orientation, record completion, and restrict personal information to authorized users. For leave or attendance changes, accept a documented request, route it to the authorized approver, update the official record, and send the employee a confirmation.

Handle complaints respectfully and confidentially, record only necessary facts, protect against retaliation, and escalate safety, harassment, or legal concerns through the designated process. Review records for access, accuracy, retention, and secure disposal under factory policy.
