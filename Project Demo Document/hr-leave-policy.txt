TEXO HR LEAVE POLICY

Department: Human Resources

Employees should submit planned leave requests to their department manager and HR before the leave begins. Emergency leave must be reported to the manager as soon as reasonably possible.

Managers should review leave requests for staffing impact and confirm approval through the approved HR process. HR maintains the final leave record and can answer questions about balances, holidays, and required documentation.

For onboarding, new employees should review this policy on Day 1 and ask HR if any leave or attendance rule is unclear.
