TEXO FACTORY KNITTING - DEPARTMENT GUIDE

Purpose and process
Knitting forms textile fabric by creating and intermeshing yarn loops. In weft knitting, yarn forms courses across the fabric; warp knitting uses yarns running along the fabric. The construction affects stretch, stability, appearance, and suitable end use, and specifications must be confirmed for each style.

Typical roles
The Knitting Manager plans machine loading; technicians set and maintain machines; operators monitor yarn supply and fabric formation; and inspectors verify construction and roll quality. Production, maintenance, and QA share machine and defect records.

Process controls
Verify yarn identity and lot, machine and gauge, construction, approved sample, and target dimensions before starting. Record authorized settings and first-off approval. Inspect at defined intervals for holes, dropped stitches, yarn contamination, and variation; identify and segregate affected rolls, document disposition, and reconcile completed quantity to the order.
