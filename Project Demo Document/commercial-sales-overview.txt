TEXO FACTORY COMMERCIAL AND SALES - DEPARTMENT GUIDE

Purpose and responsibilities
Commercial and Sales coordinates customer demand with factory capability and the documents required to execute and deliver an order. Work may include receiving buyer inquiries, preparing quotations and costing with Finance, confirming order terms, tracking approvals, and coordinating shipment documentation with logistics and compliance teams.

Typical roles
Roles can include a Sales Manager, Commercial Manager, merchandiser, costing officer, export-documentation officer, and shipment coordinator. The named owner for every order should be clear so decisions, buyer instructions, and approvals are not lost between teams.

Illustrative order workflow
Review the buyer's specification, quantity, delivery window, quality requirements, and commercial terms; record open questions; obtain internal capacity and material confirmation; issue a controlled quotation or order confirmation; and log approved changes. Track samples, production milestones, inspection status, packing information, and shipment documents against the same order reference.
